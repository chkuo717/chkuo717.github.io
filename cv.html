Research Profile

My research focuses on the design of mechanisms and robotic systems that optimise mechanical energy interactions — including those stored in springs, weights, and magnets — to enable next-generation technologies. I am globally recognised for my impact in static balancing and gravity compensation of mechanisms and robots. My research programme spans mechanism and machine design broadly, encompassing linkage synthesis, parallel manipulators, reconfigurable mechanisms, robotic actuators, rehabilitation devices, and surgical robotics. I have authored more than 100 peer-reviewed publications and hold 4 granted US patents, two of which have been transferred to industry.
