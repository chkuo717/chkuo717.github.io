Chin-Hsing Kuo · Mechanisms & Robotics

Mechanisms that carry themselves

I design mechanisms and robots that manage mechanical energy well: machines whose weight is carried by springs or magnets rather than by motors, linkages that move exactly as intended, and mechanisms that turn irregular motion into useful energy.

Senior Lecturer in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Wollongong, Australia. Fellow of ASME. Associate Editor of the ASME Journal of Mechanical Design and Mechanism and Machine Theory.

Magnetic balancing

Nearly the same, without contact

Springs wear, rub and need adjusting. Magnets can do nearly the same job without touching anything, though never perfectly: a magnet's pull does not follow the simple curve a weight does, so the balance is always approximate. At Wollongong my group designs arrangements of permanent magnets that come as close as possible: arrays that hold up a load along a long stroke, two magnets that float a load in mid-air, and rings like the one here that balance an arm and can be re-set for a different load.
