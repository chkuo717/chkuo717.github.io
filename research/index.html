Research

Four themes

Each theme page introduces the question and lists the published work under it, with a few representative papers first. Every representative paper has an animated page that computes its figure from the paper’s own geometry; smaller animations for the remaining papers will follow.

Core theme

Gravity balancing

Springs and gears arranged so that a mechanism's weight does no work at any posture: four-bar linkages, serial arms, Delta robots, reconfigurable balancers.

 Core theme

Magnetic mechanisms

Permanent-magnet arrays and Halbach cylinders as contact-free springs, long-stroke gravity compensators and passive levitators.

 Core theme

Mechanisms for renewable energy

Linkages and springs, including contact-free magnetic springs, that turn the slow, irregular motion of ocean waves and vibrating machines into a form a generator can use.

 Core theme

Mechanism theory & teaching

Relative poles for linkage synthesis and force analysis, and interactive tools for learning kinematics.

Earlier work

Research lines that are no longer active. Their pages keep the published work and its animations.

Earlier work

Structural synthesis of mechanisms

Mechanisms whose topology changes in operation: their variable joints, representations, mobility and configuration singularity; and the computational identification of equivalent links and joints in kinematic chains.

 Earlier work

Parallel & decoupled mechanisms

Structure synthesis of parallel manipulators whose motions are separated by geometry: decoupled projective motion, a non-overconstrained Agile Eye.

 Earlier work

Reconfigurable mechanisms

Mechanisms with variable topology, their mobility and configuration singularity, cube mechanisms, reconfiguration principles.

 Earlier work

Medical & rehabilitation devices

Remote-centre-of-motion mechanisms for minimally invasive surgery and craniotomy, statically balanced instrument holders, a reconfigurable balancer for lower-limb therapy.

Collaborations

Journal papers from collaborations outside the themes above.

  • Origami-inspired deployable spatial structures with terrain adaptability.
    Wang, Y., Wang, X., Qu, H., Hu, B., Kuo, C.-H. and Guo, S., Engineering Structures, 2025. doi
    Four bistable units derived from Waterbomb origami form a channel module between two panels. Snapping the units on one side from concave to convex tilts the module by about 6.78°, so a chain of modules arches, or bends into an S, to fit the terrain.
  • Curved loop strip soft gripper.
    Liu, W., Qu, H., Wang, H., Wang, X., Kuang, Y., Hu, B., Li, X., Liu, D., Kuo, C.-H. and Guo, S., International Journal of Mechanical Sciences, 2025. doi
    A loop strip is bonded at the middle into a figure-eight; rotating the middle about the long axis makes the two lobes fold down and wrap the object. Rotating the other way gives a second mode for heavier loads.
  • Design and control of a novel variable stiffness series elastic actuator.
    Sariyildiz, E., Mutlu, R., Roberts, J., Kuo, C.-H. and Ugurlu, B., IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics, 2023. doi
    The output's twist pushes the rollers against leaf springs; sliding the rollers changes the springs' working length, and with it the actuator's stiffness, from about 20 N·m/rad to about 1 kN·m/rad.
  • Compliance error compensation of a robot end-effector with joint stiffness uncertainties for milling: an analytical model.
    Nguyen, V. L., Kuo, C.-H. and Lin, P. T., Mechanism and Machine Theory, 2022. doi
    A Delta robot carried by a parked industrial arm mills along a path; joint compliance makes the cut deviate (dashed). Springs on the Delta's actuated arms, sized by the paper's formulas, reduce the deviation.
