Research

Six themes

Each theme page introduces the question and lists the published work under it, with a few representative papers first. An animated figure is being added for each paper, starting with the gravity-balancing theme; the first one is on the home page.

Core theme

Gravity balancing

Springs and gears arranged so that a mechanism's weight does no work at any posture: four-bar linkages, serial arms, Delta robots, reconfigurable balancers.

 Core theme

Magnetic mechanisms

Permanent-magnet arrays and Halbach cylinders as contact-free springs, long-stroke gravity compensators and passive levitators.

Parallel & decoupled mechanisms

Structure synthesis of parallel manipulators whose motions are separated by geometry: decoupled projective motion, a non-overconstrained Agile Eye.

Reconfigurable mechanisms

Mechanisms with variable topology, their mobility and configuration singularity, cube mechanisms, reconfiguration principles.

Medical & rehabilitation devices

Remote-centre-of-motion mechanisms for minimally invasive surgery and craniotomy, statically balanced instrument holders, a reconfigurable balancer for lower-limb therapy.

Mechanism theory & teaching

Relative poles for linkage synthesis and force analysis, structural identification of kinematic chains, and interactive tools for learning kinematics.
