Magnetic mechanisms

A pair of permanent magnets is a spring with no coil, no friction and no wear. Arranged in arrays, magnets can produce a force that follows almost any curve over a long stroke, which makes them a natural material for gravity compensation and passive levitation.

The work here combines a computational method for the force between two general magnets with design optimisation of arrays, including reconfigurable Halbach cylinders whose compensating torque can be tuned to the payload, and has been validated on built prototypes.
