Teaching

I have taught mechanical design, machine elements and the dynamics of mechanisms for fifteen years, in Taiwan, Japan, Portugal, China and Australia. At the University of Wollongong I coordinate Mechanical Design 1 (MMMB215) and Dynamics of Mechanisms (MECH326), teach in the capstone design subject Mechanical Design 2 (MECH311), and serve as Academic Program Director for Mechanical Engineering. I also teach in UOW's twinning programmes at Beijing Jiaotong University and Taiyuan University of Technology. At Taiwan Tech I coordinated the final-year capstone design course and hosted the 19th and 20th Taiwan TDK Robocon, the national collegiate robot design competition. My interactive e-book on linkage synthesis and force analysis by relative poles (GeoGebra, 2024) is open to anyone.
