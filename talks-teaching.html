Teaching

At the University of Wollongong I teach mechanical design and the kinematics and dynamics of machines, and I serve as Academic Program Director for Mechanical Engineering. In 2017 I gave a credit-bearing course, Static Balancing for Mechanisms and Robots, at the Tokyo Institute of Technology.
